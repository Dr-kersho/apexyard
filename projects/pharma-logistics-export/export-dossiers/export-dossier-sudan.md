# Sudan — pharma export dossier (Wave 1)

**PharmaConnect Egypt** · Export Wave 1 · Parallel with Libya · **First qualified buyer wins first shipment**  
**Corridor context:** CAPMAS ~**$866M** Egypt→Sudan (all sectors, 2024) · Historic **Egypt pharma supply** · Conflict-era logistics favor **Port Sudan** and **eastern states**

> **Verify before filing:** Sudan’s regulatory URLs, port control, and FX rules shift with conflict and government structure. Confirm **which NMPB office** accepts filings and **which ports** your buyer controls **before** manufacturing export batches.

---

## 1. Market snapshot

| Item | Notes |
|------|--------|
| **Demand** | Severe medicine shortages; local manufacturing (~30% of needs) disrupted since 2023 conflict |
| **Egypt angle** | Egypt listed among **licensed import origins**; firms shifted to **overland import from Egypt** when Khartoum production stopped |
| **Import dependency** | Majority of finished medicines imported; NMPB licensed **150+** companies (pre-conflict baseline) |
| **v1 opportunity** | Essential generics, chronic therapies, hospital basics — **oral forms first** |
| **v1 constraint** | **FX, payment, and last-mile security** — higher risk than Libya on treasury, not necessarily on registration science |

## 2. Regulatory authorities (map)

| Body | Role | Notes |
|------|------|--------|
| **National Medicines and Poisons Board (NMPB)** | Marketing authorization, GMP oversight, pharmacovigilance | Primary regulator |
| **National General Directorate of Pharmacy (NGDOP)** | Executive arm — registration processing, inspections | Day-to-day filings |
| **Federal Ministry of Health** | Policy | Parent ministry |
| **National Medical Supplies Fund (NMSF)** | Public-sector procurement | Separate tender path — not v1 unless invited |
| **Customs at authorized ports** | Import sampling & testing | Release after port QC |

**Website (verify live):** `http://www.nmpb.gov.sd/` — if down, work through importer’s RA contact in **Port Sudan** or **Khartoum** (if operational).

## 3. Product registration pathway (foreign manufacturer)

### 3.1 Legal basis

- **Pharmacy and Poisons Act** — offence to import/sell unregistered products.
- All applicants must meet **Federal Board of Pharmacy / NGDOP** requirements.

### 3.2 Manufacturer registration (before or with product)

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| Company registration file | Application, manufacturing licence, GMP, ISO if devices |
| Site inspection | NMPB may inspect foreign site or rely on **WHO GMP** + CPP |
| Authorisation in other countries | List of markets where product is approved |

### 3.3 Product dossier (typical)

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Format | **CTD**-style modules |
| **CPP** | WHO certificate scheme — from **EDA Egypt** |
| **GMP certificate** | Manufacturing site |
| SLSPP / licensing status | As per Sudan templates |
| Efficacy & safety data | SmPC, PIL, BE studies for generics |
| **Pricing** | **C&F price proposed** — NMPB **price committee** approval (certificate shows approved price) |
| Validity | Registration certificate commonly **5 years** — renewal with updated CPP |

### 3.4 Authentication chain (critical path)

Documents from Egypt generally require:

1. Notarization / authentication per Egyptian procedure  
2. **Sudanese Embassy in Cairo** stamp  
3. **Sudan Ministry of Foreign Affairs** (as specified in published checklists)

**Lead time:** Add **3–6 weeks** for embassy/legalization stack in critical path.

### 3.5 Timelines (planning assumptions)

| Phase | Planning range | Risk |
|-------|----------------|------|
| Dossier prep | 8–16 weeks | Same as Libya if same CDMO |
| NMPB assessment | **≤12 months** (statutory reference in country profiles) | Queries, sample testing |
| Port release testing | +2–6 weeks per shipment | Lab backlog |
| **Accelerator** | Importer with existing Egyptian generic registrations | Faster variation |

## 4. Import & customs (per shipment)

### 4.1 Authorized entry (confirm current)

| Mode | Corridor | Notes |
|------|----------|--------|
| **Sea** | **Port Sudan** (Red Sea) | Primary hub post-conflict for international arrivals |
| **Land** | **Argeen**, **Qustul** (Egypt–Sudan border) | Used for Egyptian overland pharma replenishment |
| **Air** | Khartoum / Port Sudan | Urgent/high-value small lots — cost prohibitive for v1 |

**Distribution reality (2024–2025 literature):** Importers may reach **eastern states** (Kassala, Gedaref, River Nile, Northern) and **Port Sudan** more reliably than **Khartoum / Darfur / Kordofan**. Contract must name **delivery territory**.

### 4.2 Import process

```
Arrival at authorized port
        ↓
Customs declaration + invoice pack
        ↓
NMPB port sampling & laboratory testing
        ↓
Release to importer GDP warehouse
        ↓
Wholesale distribution (territory-limited)
```

### 4.3 Per-shipment documents

| Document | Notes |
|----------|--------|
| Commercial invoice & packing list | Match registered C&F price band |
| **CoA** (batch) | Egyptian QP release |
| **CPP** copy & registration certificate | Product must match registered presentation |
| Import licence / release authorization | Per consignment — importer obtains |
| GDP / cold-chain records | If temperature-sensitive |
| Bill of lading | Sea or land CMR |

## 5. Logistics from Alexandria warehouse

```
[Alexandria GDP warehouse]
        │
        ├─► Truck → Salloum area → Argeen / Qustul → importer (land)
        │
        └─► Truck → Suez/Red Sea port → sea → Port Sudan
```

| Control | Action |
|---------|--------|
| Land route | Faster for replenishment; **heat & security** risk — night driving policy, escort if importer provides |
| Sea route | Better for pallet volume; longer lead time |
| Conflict routing | Avoid routing through non-controlled areas — importer defines safe corridor |
| Insurance | **Political risk + cargo** essential |

## 6. Payment & FX (higher risk than Libya)

| Topic | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Currency** | Invoice in **USD**; local settlement often subject to **parallel rates** |
| **Instruments** | Prefer **LC at reputable bank**, **advance via correspondent bank**, or **escrow** — avoid unverified middlemen |
| **Public sector** | NMSF tenders — long payment cycles; **not v1** unless pre-funded |
| **PharmaConnect rule** | Minimum **30–50% advance** or LC at sight unless CEO exception with bank comfort letter |

**Red flags:** “Pay in cash in Cairo,” no bank name, discount for skipping CoA, request to misdeclare HS code.

## 7. Buyer qualification (Sudan-specific)

| Must-have | Why |
|-----------|-----|
| Valid NMPB **import licence** | Legal import |
| Warehouse in **reachable territory** | Port Sudan / east if Khartoum unsafe |
| **Port clearance** track record | Avoid first-shipment learning on your batch |
| RA staff or consultant | Embassy auth chain handled routinely |
| Named **bank** for LC | Not personal accounts |

## 8. Pricing & margin (model)

| Element | Guidance |
|---------|----------|
| Incoterm | **CIF Port Sudan** or **CIP importer warehouse** (land) |
| NMPB pricing | Register **C&F** low enough to win tenders but protect margin — price changes need **NMPB approval** |
| FX buffer | Add **5–15%** FX contingency to working capital model |
| Target gross margin | **40–60%** (internal model) if payment collected |

**Note:** Historical studies flagged imported generics with **high C&F vs reference prices** — competitive pricing still matters despite shortages.

## 9. Risk register

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Conflict / route closure | Dual route quotes (land + sea); territory clause |
| FX / payment default | Advance or LC; credit insurance if available |
| Port lab delay | Importer pre-clearance relationships; buffer stock |
| Fake importer | Bank KYC + site visit |
| Khartoum unreachable | East-only distribution contract |
| Embassy backlog | Start legalization parallel to dossier |

## 11. Conflict & humanitarian note

Sudan’s health system is under **severe stress**. Commercial exports must **not** circumvent sanctions or humanitarian corridors your legal counsel flags. Keep donations separate from commercial SKU flow.
