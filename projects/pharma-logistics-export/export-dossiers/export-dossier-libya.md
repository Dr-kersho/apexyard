# Libya — pharma export dossier (Wave 1)

**PharmaConnect Egypt** · Export Wave 1 · Parallel with Sudan · **First qualified buyer wins first shipment**  
**Corridor context:** CAPMAS ~**$2.0B** Egypt→Libya (all sectors, 2024) · Alexandria warehouse → Mediterranean ports / **Salloum** land crossing

> **Verify before filing:** Dual government (Tripoli GNU vs eastern authorities), CBL circulars, and NMPB/FDCC naming are in flux. Confirm port of entry and which authority accepts your dossier **before** booking freight.

---

## 1. Market snapshot

| Item | Notes |
|------|--------|
| **Demand** | High import dependency; chronic shortages in public and private channels |
| **Egypt angle** | Geographic proximity from Alexandria; established informal/formal trade corridor |
| **Pharma-specific** | Finished generics and essential medicines; cold chain for vaccines/biologics (harder v1) |
| **Competition** | EU, Tunisia, Turkey, India, other Arab producers — **WHO ML3 (Egypt, Dec 2024)** is a differentiation story for quality acceptance |
| **v1 SKU profile** | Oral solids / liquids with long shelf life, high turnover, already EDA-registered |

---

## 2. Regulatory authorities (map)

| Body | Role | Notes |
|------|------|--------|
| **Ministry of Health (MOH)** | Health policy | Parent ministry |
| **National Medicines and Poisons Board (NMPB)** | Product registration, ICH-aligned dossier review | Primary **marketing authorization** path |
| **Food and Drug Control Centre (FDCC)** | Import clearance for medicines & medical devices | Often cited for **pre-import approval** linked to **CBL** payment |
| **Central Bank of Libya (CBL)** | FX, LCs for commercial imports | Medical imports may need **FDCC approval before LC** |
| **Libyan Customs + ACI** | Pre-shipment cargo registration | **Mandatory since Nov 2024** for containerized imports |

**Practical split:** Plan for **two tracks** — (1) product registration with **NMPB**, (2) import shipment approval **FDCC + customs ACI + CBL payment** per consignment.

## 3. Product registration pathway (foreign manufacturer)

### 3.1 Prerequisites

- **Manufacturing site** registered or accepted by NMPB (may require **site questionnaire** and/or inspection).
- **GMP proof** (WHO-type certificate; Egyptian site with EDA GMP + **WHO ML3** narrative helps).
- **Dossier** structured per **ICH CTD** modules (quality, non-clinical, clinical as applicable).
- **CPP** from Egypt (**EDA**, WHO certificate scheme format).
- **Labeling** and Arabic package insert per Libyan requirements.
- **Stability** data for zone IVb or justified storage conditions.

### 3.2 Typical dossier contents (checklist)

| Module / doc | Content |
|--------------|---------|
| Administrative | Application form, POA for local agent, manufacturing licence copy |
| Quality | Drug substance & product specs, batch formula, validation, stability |
| Clinical | SmPC, PIL, bioequivalence if generic |
| Certificates | CPP, GMP, Free Sale, GLP/GCP where relevant |
| Samples | May be requested — plan retention at Alexandria warehouse |

### 3.3 Timelines (planning assumptions)

| Phase | Planning range | Risk |
|-------|----------------|------|
| Dossier prep (from EDA-ready product) | 8–16 weeks | CDMO docs incomplete |
| NMPB review | 6–18 months | Queue, queries, inspection |
| FDCC import permit (per SKU or company) | 4–12 weeks | Parallel with registration strategy |
| **Accelerator** | Importer with **existing registered line** + add-your-manufacturer variation | Depends on importer RA |

**v1 tactic:** Partner with importer who already registers Egyptian lines — file **variation** or **new product** under their portfolio while you build direct MAH later.

## 4. Import & customs (per shipment)

### 4.1 Serialization & labeling

- **GS1 DataMatrix** on sellable units (GTIN, serial, batch, expiry) per Libyan pharma serialization rules (MoE Decision 379/2023 framework — confirm GTIN issuance).
- **Arabic** on commercial invoice and key customs documents.

### 4.2 Advance Cargo Information (ACI)

| Step | Requirement |
|------|-------------|
| Portal | Importer + exporter register at **aci.customs.gov.ly** |
| Timing | **ACI number ≥48 hours before vessel loading** |
| B/L | ACI number **on bill of lading** — carriers enforce no-load without it |
| Scope | Containerized commercial cargo (confirm LCL vs FCL with forwarder) |

### 4.3 Other import documents

| Document | Notes |
|----------|--------|
| Commercial invoice + packing list | Arabic translation |
| **Certificate of Origin** | Chamber of Commerce + **legalization at Libyan embassy in Egypt before dispatch** |
| Bill of lading / airway bill | ACI embedded |
| **FDCC approval** | Medicines / devices — before clearance and often before **CBL LC** |
| Insurance | Match Incoterms |
| CoA per batch | From Egyptian manufacturer / release QP |

### 4.4 Ports & corridors (from Alexandria)

| Route | Entry | Best for | Caution |
|-------|-------|----------|---------|
| **Sea** | **Misrata**, **Tripoli** (Khoms) | Palletized cargo, west Libya | GNU jurisdiction; ACI enforced |
| **Sea** | **Benghazi**, Tobruk | East Libya | **Different authority** — confirm FDCC/NMPB acceptance |
| **Land** | **Salloum** → Libyan border | Urgent replenishment, smaller lots | Security, border queues, temperature control in summer |

**Recommendation:** Align **port of entry** with importer’s warehouse and the government they routinely clear through — do not assume west = east interchangeability.

### 4.5 Clearance time (planning)

| Scenario | Days (indicative) |
|----------|-------------------|
| ACI + docs complete, no LC hold | 5–10 |
| FDCC + CBL LC for pharma | 7–14+ |
| Documentation error | Shipment may **not load** at origin |

## 5. Payment & FX

| Topic | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **CBL** | Commercial imports typically require **CBL-approved banking**; informal cash settlement not viable at scale |
| **Letter of Credit** | Common for larger pharma lots; **FDCC approval may precede LC** |
| **Thresholds** | CBL circulars change — confirm with importer’s bank before proforma |
| **Currency** | Invoice in **USD**; confirm conversion rules with buyer bank |
| **PharmaConnect rule** | **No shipment without agreed payment instrument** (LC, advance, or escrow with bank counsel sign-off) |

**Risk:** LC delays can strand cargo at port — build **demurrage** clause and max storage days in contract.

## 7. Buyer qualification (Libya-specific)

| Must-have | Why |
|-----------|-----|
| Valid **wholesale import licence** | Legal import |
| **FDCC** interaction history | Clears pharma without last-minute blocks |
| **CBL banking relationship** | Can open/process LC |
| **ACI portal** competency | Avoid no-load at Alexandria |
| West **or** east coverage matching your port choice | Avoid wrong-government clearance |

**Red flags:** Cash-only settlement promises, no fixed warehouse, cannot name clearing broker, refuses GDP handover docs.

| Landed cost | CIF + 4% port services tax framework + category taxes — **model per HS code** |
| Target gross margin | Plan **40–60%** on export COGS (internal model) |
| Working capital | LC float + registration amortization across SKUs |

## 9. Risk register

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Dual government / port mismatch | Lock east vs west in contract |
| ACI miss → no load | Importer generates ACI before you book vessel |
| CBL / LC delay | FDCC pre-approval; partial advance if bank allows |
| COO not legalized pre-dispatch | Embassy timeline in critical path |
| Counterfeit perception | WHO ML3 + visit Alexandria warehouse + serialisation |
| Security (land route) | Prefer sea for first shipment |
