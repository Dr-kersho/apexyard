## 0. Three financial buckets (investor view)

| Bucket | Arabic | What it is | In monthly P&L |
|--------|--------|------------|----------------|
| **① Opex** | مصروف تشغيل | Salaries, insurance, utilities, security — **cost to run the warehouse** | Operating expense |
| **② COGS** | تكلفة البضاعة | Invoices paid to **buy medicines** for resale | Cost of goods sold |
| **③ Hero trade margin** | دعم تجاري على المنتج | Selling 50 hero SKUs at **lower margin** to win share — **not a salary line** | Reduces **gross profit** (or offset by factory rebate) |

### Hero subsidy — explained for the plan

**What it is NOT:** A monthly “operations” bill like rent or payroll.

**What it IS:** A **commercial decision on product pricing**. You buy a SKU at cost **C**, normally sell at **P** (margin = P − C). On heroes you sell at **P′ < P** → each unit “costs” you **(P − P′)** in **forgone gross profit**.

**Example**

| | Normal | Hero promo |
|--|--------|------------|
| Cost (C) | 100 EGP | 100 EGP |
| Sell price | 115 EGP (15% margin) | 108 EGP (8% margin) |
| **Subsidy per unit sold** | — | **7 EGP** |

If hero lines sell **500,000 EGP/month** and average extra discount is **2% of sales** → margin give-up ≈ **10,000 EGP/month** — booked under **gross margin**, not opex.

**Cap in the plan:** Set a **maximum monthly margin give-up** (e.g. 80–150k EGP) reviewed weekly — same discipline as inventory budget.

**Later:** Manufacturers may pay **trade marketing rebates** → net subsidy **falls** (reduces effective COGS).

**Delivery:** Couriers on **own motorcycles** @ **6k/mo** — **no van fleet**. Small fuel/box allowance ~**4k/mo** total.

## 2. What QA/GDP officer does (why you need this hire)

**You cannot be full-time CEO + pharmacist + QA/GDP on the floor** once orders scale. EDA **GDP wholesale** expects documented quality systems even if you sign as Responsible Pharmacist.

| Function | **QA** (Quality Assurance) | **GDP** (Good Distribution Practice) |
|----------|------------------------------|--------------------------------------|
| **Purpose** | Prove every batch sold is **approved and traceable** | Prove storage & transport **protect product quality** |
| **Daily work** | Match batch numbers to CoA; quarantine suspect stock; handle pharmacy complaints | Temperature logs (room + cold); FEFO/FIFO; clean zones; pest control records |
| **Documents** | SOPs, deviation reports, recall plan | GDP self-inspection, mapping, calibration |
| **Release** | Supports **batch release** before pick (with Responsible Pharmacist) | Ensures pick/pack + handover to couriers meets GDP |
| **Audits** | Prepares evidence for EDA inspection | Walks auditors through warehouse zones |
| **Export link** | Batch files for **CoA** in Libya/Sudan shipments | Cold chain evidence for international dossiers |

**One hire @ ~15k EGP/mo** often covers **QA + GDP coordinator** at this size; split into two roles only if EDA or volume demands it.

**Owner (pharmacist):** Legal signatory, registration, policy approval — **not** every temperature log.

## 6. Break-even sanity (opex ~523k/mo)

Assume **12% blended product gross margin** (after hero give-up), **AOV 1,100 EGP**, **delivery fee** separate:

| To cover opex from product margin alone | Value |
|----------------------------------------|-------|
| Gross profit needed | ~523,000 EGP/mo |
| GMV @ 12% margin | **~4.36M EGP/mo** |
| Orders @ 1,100 AOV | **~3,440 orders/mo** |

| Scenario | Orders/mo | Comment |
|----------|-----------|---------|
| 100 pharmacies × 2 orders | 200 | **Far below** break-even on ops alone |
| 100 pharmacies × 6 orders | 600 | GM ~79k — partial |
| 300 pharmacies × 4 orders | 1,200 | GM ~158k + delivery fees + runway |

**Investor message:** Funded launch opex is **~523k EGP/mo fixed** (incl. founder **55k**); path to break-even = **order frequency**, **margin mix**, **delivery fee**, and **GMV ramp** in months 6–12 — not cutting to a “pilot” headcount.
