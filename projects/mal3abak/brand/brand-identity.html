04 — Brand Voice

Confident. Precise. Motivating.

We speak like a coach who has the data — direct, no fluff. We believe in the athlete before the world does. We never condescend; we never oversell.

Confident
"Your next signing is already on the pitch."
"توقيعك القادم موجود بالفعل في الملعب"
Precise
"94th-percentile pressing rate. Age 14."
"معدل ضغط في الشريحة 94. عمره 14 سنة"
Motivating
"Every field is a scouting ground."
"كل ملعب هو فرصة اكتشاف"

We say

✓
"Scout smarter" — action-led, implies edge
✓
"Discover talent before the crowd" — aspirational, specific
✓
"Your field, your data" — ownership, empowerment
✓
"Built for the next generation" — forward, inclusive

We never say

✕
"Leverage synergies" — corporate filler
✕
"AI-powered solution" — vague, overused
✕
"Disrupting the industry" — empty
✕
"World-class" without evidence — claim without proof

05 — Logo Usage Rules

What to do — and what never to do.

Always

✓
Use the provided SVG files — never redraw from memory
✓
Maintain minimum clear space: half the badge height on all sides
✓
Use full-colour on dark grounds, reversed on white grounds
✓
At sizes below 48px wide, use the icon-only mark
✓
Keep the "3" in Signal Orange (#FF6B00) at all times

Never

✕
Stretch, squash, or rotate the mark
✕
Place on a busy photographic background without a safe zone
✕
Change any colour in the full-colour version
✕
Add drop shadows, gradients, or effects to the wordmark
✕
Use the wordmark at sizes below 120px wide
